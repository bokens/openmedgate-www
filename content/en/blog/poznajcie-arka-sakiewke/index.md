*The third episode of the “One bid” series. The result of the name contest and a preview of the episodes to come.*

A week ago our supplier of hospital information systems had no name. Today he has a first name, a surname and a new office. He still does not exist, but he is now complete.

## The first name

Arek. From A.R.E.K., which stands for API Requires Expensive Keys. The acronym and the diminutive “Pan Areczek” came in two separate comments, and only together did they make a name.

## The surname

Sakiewka, Polish for a coin purse. Because somebody has to pay for every key. Guess who.

Thank you for all the proposals. Interfejsław Zgodowicz and Janusz HISzPAŃSKI came very close.

## The office

Arek has already settled in and proudly shows his office. Please note the wall behind him. Through the glass door you can see a hospital corridor.

## What comes next

In the next episode we will see what Arek does when a buyer admits an “equivalent solution” but has no description of the interfaces that the solution has to be connected to.

A reminder: Arek Sakiewka does not exist, and his pictures are generated by AI. The documents I write about do.
