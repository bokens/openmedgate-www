## A standard and an installation's specification

A standard says what data should look like on the way between systems. It does not say at which address, with which authentication and in which scope a specific installation exposes that data. That is described by the installation's specification, and that is what is licensed.

## Three clauses that change the situation

1. The interface description is held by the hospital and may be handed to a contractor of the hospital's choice.
2. The terms of access to the interfaces are set on the day the contract is signed, not on the day of the first integration.
3. The hospital decides about the test environment.
