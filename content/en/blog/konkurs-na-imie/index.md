*The second episode of the “One bid” series. I announced the contest on LinkedIn at the end of October 2026; this is how it went.*

## Where this gentleman came from

In the first episode, next to a doctor with a blocked screen, stood a stout man in a three-piece suit, with a badge reading “HIS supplier” and an oversized golden key around his neck. The key opens the interfaces of the system the hospital bought and works on every day. The hospital has no such key.

The character is fictional and his pictures are generated by AI. He depicts no company and no person. He does exactly what the procurement documents show: he holds the key, he does not hand out the specification, and he is very pleased with himself.

## The contest

On the badge, in place of a name, a question mark appeared. I asked for proposals in the comments. One condition applied. The name must not bring to mind a real company or a real person in the industry, because the character is a caricature of a practice and depicts nobody in particular.

## What came in

There were more proposals than I expected. Some played on the name of the system, some on interfaces, some on the key. Among them, in Polish: Interfejsław Zgodowicz, Janusz HISzPAŃSKI, Hieronim Ireneusz Sakiewka and Pan Areczek, plus an acronym, A.R.E.K., which stands for API Requires Expensive Keys.

## The verdict

The verdict came on 29 October. The first name and the surname were put together from two proposals, and I announced them in the [next episode](/blog/meet-arek-sakiewka/), together with the character's new office.
