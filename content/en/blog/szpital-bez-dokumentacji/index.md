## What the documents say

The question concerned one thing: the description of the interfaces of the system that a new module has to integrate with. The buyer's answer is that it does not hold such documentation, because it is the proprietary documentation of the producer.

> [Placeholder for a quotation from the document, with page number and date.]

## What follows

The buyer admitted an equivalent solution and listed six equivalence criteria. None of them can be met without access to the interface description, which the buyer does not have and cannot hand over.

One bid was submitted.

## What does not follow

Nobody's fault follows from this. The buyer answered the questions, changed the documents and lowered the participation threshold. It did what it could. The wall stands elsewhere: in the contract that governs access to documentation that already exists.
