## The unit

We count lots of a contract, not whole procedures. One procedure may have several lots with different numbers of bids, and averaging them into one number loses exactly what we want to see.

## Window and sources

Twenty-four months of result notices. Below the EU thresholds the source is the national bulletin; above them, TED.

## Reading the documents

Every document is hashed when it is captured. Every document is read twice, the second time by someone who does not know the first reading. A discrepancy is not averaged away; it is recorded and explained.

## What we do not do

We do not estimate a budget when the document does not state one. We record “no information”.
